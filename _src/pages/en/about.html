SINCE 2016

It All Started with One Co-Living Apartment

The story of The Meeting Place began with a single co-living apartment in Taipei.

At first, our goal was simple: create a comfortable place where people could settle in and feel at home.

But as we began operating the space ourselves, we realized that a good home is about much more than having a bed, a desk, and Wi-Fi.

Finding the right residents, preparing the space, managing move-ins, handling maintenance, and creating a comfortable shared-living environment all matter.

That experience gradually led us from co-living into rental management and space improvement.

We take care of the apartment, so the people living in it can simply live well.

That has remained at the heart of what we do.

Four Things We Care About

RESPONSIVENESS

You always know who to turn to.

From move-in information to everyday questions, we believe good management starts with clear communication and reliable support. When something happens, both residents and property owners should know what comes next.

TRUST

Your property stays clear and manageable.

Every property is an important asset. Through transparent management, regular communication, and ongoing maintenance, we make sure property owners can stay informed even when daily management is in our hands.

COMFORT

A place where people can live well.

A home is more than a place to sleep. We care about safety, comfort, shared spaces, and everyday living so that people from different backgrounds can find a rhythm that works for them.

CARE

Good spaces need ongoing attention.

From everyday upkeep and equipment checks to repairs when needed, we continue looking after each apartment over time. Because a well-managed home should stay comfortable — not just look good on move-in day.
