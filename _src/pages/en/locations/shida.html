Before Moving into Shida Apartment, You May Want to Know

Is Shida Apartment suitable for Mandarin learners and international students?

Yes. Shida Apartment is located near NTNU and the Mandarin-learning community, making it one of our most suitable locations for students and international residents.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 30 days. Actual availability depends on your move-in date, room type and length of stay.

Is it convenient to get from Shida Apartment to NTNU?

Yes. Shida Apartment is located in the Shida neighborhood, close to NTNU and Taipower Building MRT Station.

Can I cook?

Yes. A shared kitchen with basic cooking equipment is available.

Do male and female residents share the same bedroom?

No. Bedrooms are arranged by gender. Common areas are shared by all residents.

How can I check current availability?

Send us your move-in date, expected length of stay, gender and number of residents, and we'll help you check suitable rooms.

Coming to Taipei to Study or Learn Mandarin?

Tell us your school, expected move-in date and length of stay, and we'll help you check whether Shida Apartment currently has a suitable room for you.
