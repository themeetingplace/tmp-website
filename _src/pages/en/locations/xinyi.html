A Quiet Place to Come Home to in the Middle of the City

Located in Taipei's Xinyi area, Xinyi Apartment feels more like a quiet and relaxed shared home than a large, lively co-living community.

The common areas are bright and comfortable, and the bedrooms receive good natural light. After work or class, you can relax in the living room or return to your room and enjoy a calmer daily routine.

If convenient transportation matters to you but you also want somewhere peaceful to come home to, Xinyi Apartment is well suited for longer-term living.

Xinyi Apartment is a good fit if you...

  • Study or work around Xinyi, Nangang or central Taipei
  • Value easy MRT access and convenient commuting

Before Moving into Xinyi Apartment, You May Want to Know

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 30 days. Availability depends on your move-in date and length of stay.

Is Xinyi Apartment suitable for working professionals?

Yes. Its quieter environment and convenient transportation make it particularly suitable for residents with regular work commutes.

Can international residents stay here?

Yes. We regularly welcome international students and overseas residents.

Can I cook?

Yes. A shared kitchen and basic cooking equipment are available.

Do male and female residents share the same bedroom?

No. Bedrooms are arranged by gender, while common areas are shared by residents.

How can I check availability?

Send us your move-in date, expected length of stay, gender and number of residents, and we'll help you check current availability.
