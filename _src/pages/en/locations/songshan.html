Feel at Home in a Social Co-Living Community

Songshan Apartment is one of our largest co-living locations and has the strongest sense of community among our apartments.

With a spacious living room, shared dining table and multiple common areas, there is plenty of room to work, eat, relax or spend time with roommates. You may come home to people chatting on the sofa, working at the table, having dinner together — or even one of our dogs waiting to greet you.

If you want your life in Taipei to be more than simply renting a bed and closing your bedroom door, Songshan Apartment may be a great place for you.

Songshan Apartment is a good fit if you...

  • Enjoy a lively co-living environment and interacting with roommates
  • Prefer a spacious living room, dining area and larger common spaces
  • Study or work around Songshan, Xinyi or Nangang
  • Enjoy meeting people from different backgrounds
  • Like dogs and enjoy having animals around in everyday life

Before Moving into Songshan Apartment, You May Want to Know

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 30 days. Please send us your expected move-in date and length of stay to check availability.

Can international students stay here?

Yes. We regularly welcome international students, exchange students and Mandarin learners coming to Taipei.

Do male and female residents share the same bedroom?

No. Bedrooms are arranged by gender. Common areas are shared by all residents.

Can I cook?

Yes. A shared kitchen and basic cooking equipment are available.

Are there dogs at Songshan Apartment?

Yes. Dogs are part of daily life at Songshan Apartment. If you are allergic to or uncomfortable around dogs, please let us know before booking.

How can I check availability?

Send us your expected move-in date, length of stay, gender and number of residents, and we'll help you check suitable room options.
