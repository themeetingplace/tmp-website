From Leasing to Everyday Management, We Help Handle the Details

Good property management is about more than simply finding a tenant.
Depending on the property, location, and your goals, we assess different rental and management approaches — including rental management, master leasing, shared rentals, and co-living — rather than applying the same model to every property.

01｜Property Rental Assessment

We review the current condition, layout, facilities, and your goals to determine suitable rental and management options.

02｜Rental Positioning & Space Planning

Based on the location, property condition, and local demand, we assess whether the property is better suited for whole-unit rental, shared rental, co-living, or improvements before entering the market.

03｜Marketing & Tenant Screening

We help market the property, arrange viewings, explain house rules, and screen prospective tenants to reduce vacancy and future management risks.

04｜Lease Management & Tenant Communication

We handle rent reminders, tenant communication, house rules, and everyday rental matters to reduce the amount of time owners need to spend managing the property.

05｜Maintenance & Repairs

When equipment, plumbing, electrical systems, or other maintenance issues arise, we help coordinate, follow up, and manage the necessary work.

06｜Regular Updates & Transparent Records

We organize rental status, maintenance records, property issues, and management progress so owners can clearly understand what is happening with their property.
