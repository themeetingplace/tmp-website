Before Moving into Guting 1 Apartment, You May Want to Know

Is Guting 1 Apartment suitable for remote work?

Yes. The apartment includes spaces suitable for working and using a laptop, along with a generally calmer living atmosphere.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 30 days. Actual availability depends on your move-in date and current room availability.

Is Guting 1 Apartment suitable for students?

Yes. Guting is conveniently located near NTNU, NTU and several other campuses, with easy access to transportation and everyday amenities.

Can I cook?

Yes. A shared kitchen with basic cooking equipment is available.

Do male and female residents share the same bedroom?

No. Bedrooms are arranged by gender, while common areas are shared by all residents.
