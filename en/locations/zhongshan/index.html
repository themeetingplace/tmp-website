Before Moving into Zhongshan Apartment, You May Want to Know

Is Zhongshan Apartment suitable for international residents?

Yes. Zhongshan Apartment has a diverse mix of residents and is particularly suitable for international students and overseas residents who would like to meet people from different backgrounds.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 30 days. Availability depends on your move-in date and length of stay.

Is Zhongshan Apartment close to the MRT?

Yes. It is near Xingtian Temple MRT Station, making it convenient to travel around central Taipei.

Can I cook?

Yes. A shared kitchen and basic cooking equipment are available.

Do male and female residents share the same bedroom?

No. Bedrooms are arranged by gender. Common areas are shared.
