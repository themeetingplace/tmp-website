A Few Things to Know Before You Move In

01｜We don't offer same-day move-in

Rooms need to be prepared, confirmed and made ready, so please reach out early if you have a move-in timeline.

02｜We check fit before confirming

To keep the co-living experience comfortable and safe for everyone, we'll ask about your basic needs and lifestyle before confirming a suitable location and room.

03｜This is a co-living space

Co-living means sharing some common areas, along with mutual respect, keeping shared spaces tidy and following house guidelines.

If you're looking for a fully private space with no shared areas, The Meeting Place may not be the best fit for you.
